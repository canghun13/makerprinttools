Production planning

3D Print Farm Capacity Calculator

Separate installed time from the time your farm is likely to be available and doing useful production work.

Inputs

Time the printers are able to run as planned.
Share of available time assigned to productive printing.

Result

—

Calculation purpose and formula

Theoretical printer-hours = printers × scheduled hours/day × days. Availability-adjusted hours then apply availability; productive hours apply utilization after that.

Interpret the result

Use the three layers together when checking whether a quoted delivery window has enough capacity. Availability covers outages and maintenance; utilization covers waiting, changeovers, and deliberately unassigned time.

Worked example

Four printers scheduled for 16 hours across seven days offer 448 theoretical printer-hours. At 90% availability and 80% productive utilization, the plan has 322.56 expected productive hours.

Assumptions and limitations

Each printer-hour is treated as equivalent. For mixed printers, convert each machine to compatible workload hours before combining them. This is a planning capacity estimate, not a prediction of individual job finish times.

Common planning mistake

Do not multiply only one generic efficiency percentage and call it utilization: downtime and idle or turnaround time point to different operational fixes.
