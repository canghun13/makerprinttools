Production planning

Print Farm Planning Tools

Turn printer-hours, availability, utilization, yield, and deadlines into an operating plan you can inspect.

Plan capacity before the queue becomes urgent

These tools separate the time installed in a farm from the time that is available, productively scheduled, and converted into good units. Use the calculator that matches the decision: whether to quote a deadline, clear a backlog, investigate idle time, or plan recurring output.

3D Print Farm Capacity Calculator
Calculate theoretical, availability-adjusted, and productive printer-hours for a 3D print farm schedule.Required Printer Count Calculator
Estimate how many compatible 3D printers are required to deliver a batch by a deadline after yield and capacity losses.Print Queue Completion Time Calculator
Estimate operating days to clear a 3D print queue using compatible printer count, availability, utilization, and turnaround allowance.Printer Utilization Calculator
Calculate gross capacity, maintenance-adjusted capacity, productive utilization, idle time, and unaccounted printer-hours.Print Farm Downtime Impact Calculator
Estimate the printer-hours, print cycles, and units a 3D print farm loses to expected downtime.Batch Throughput Calculator
Estimate daily, weekly, and monthly 3D print farm throughput from cycle time, capacity losses, and expected yield.
