Support Material Estimator

Estimate support material mass and allowance from supported volume, density, interface overhead, and a material-loss reserve.

Result

—

How the calculation works

Estimated mass = support volume × density × (1 + interface allowance % ÷ 100) × (1 + reserve % ÷ 100). Combined allowance % = interface % + reserve % + (interface % × reserve % ÷ 100).

How to use this result

Use measured or slicer-reported support volume where possible. Allowances apply in sequence: loss reserve applies to the base support mass plus the interface/purge addition. If the slicer volume already includes that material, enter 0 for the interface/purge allowance to avoid counting it twice. Verify the final support generation in your slicer.

Worked example

100 cm³ at 1 g/cm³ gives 100 g of base support material. A 20% interface/purge allowance adds 20 g. A 10% reserve on that 120 g subtotal adds 12 g, giving 132 g total: a combined 32% allowance, not 30%.
