What this tool calculates

Turn caliper or sensor readings into a reproducible QC summary instead of judging a spool from one point.

Formula and method

With paired axes, equivalent circular diameter = √(d₁ × d₂), preserving measured cross-sectional area. The checker reports arithmetic mean, population standard deviation, min/max, tolerance pass rate, and ovality = |d₁ − d₂| ÷ mean(d₁,d₂) × 100%.

When to use it

Measure along a spool at consistent intervals. For manual checks, rotate the filament roughly 90° at each position and enter the paired cross-axis readings.

Worked example

The default eight paired samples average about 1.751 mm equivalent diameter, all remain within 1.70–1.80 mm, and maximum ovality is about 1.14%.

Assumptions and limitations

Readings are finite positive millimetre values from a calibrated instrument, and paired lists refer to the same positions. A small sample cannot certify an entire spool. Measurement force, temperature, sensor calibration, sampling interval, and local defects remain important.

Common mistake

Do not average two axes first and discard ovality; an acceptable mean can hide a strand that is too wide in one direction.
